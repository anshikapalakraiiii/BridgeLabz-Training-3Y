Local News Round-Up

The city council approved a new budget yesterday evening after a lengthy session that stretched past midnight. Residents packed the chamber to voice opinions on proposed road improvements and park renovations planned for the coming year.

Local business owners expressed cautious optimism about the plan, noting that improved infrastructure could draw more foot traffic to the downtown corridor. Several attendees, however, raised concerns about the timeline and possible disruptions during construction.

Council members say the first phase of work will begin in early spring, with public updates scheduled monthly. A dedicated hotline has been set up for residents to report concerns during construction.

In related news, the community library announced extended weekend hours starting next month, a change many parents and students have requested for over a year. The decision followed a survey showing strong demand for weekend study space.

Meanwhile, the annual harvest festival returns this weekend with expanded vendor stalls, live music, and a farmers market featuring produce from over thirty local growers across the region.
