Kyoto, Japan

Overview Culture Food Travel Tips

Overview

Kyoto served as Japan's capital for over a thousand years and remains one of the country's best-preserved historic cities, home to thousands of temples, shrines, and traditional wooden houses.

Culture

From tea ceremonies to geisha districts, Kyoto's cultural traditions are still practiced today. Gion is the most famous historic district, known for its narrow lanes and traditional teahouses.

Food

Kyoto cuisine, known as Kyo-ryori, emphasizes seasonal ingredients and delicate presentation. Don't miss yudofu (hot tofu) and matcha sweets from local shops.

Travel Tips

Visit in spring for cherry blossoms or autumn for fall colors. A prepaid IC card makes getting around by bus and train simple.
